A full spectrum of care, under one roof.

Dental Care

Meet Your Vet

Trusted local care, led by Dr. Niki Lagasse.

Dr. Lagasse purchased Newburyport Veterinary Clinic in 2015 with one goal: build the kind of practice she’d trust with her own pets. A decade later, that promise still drives every appointment — from a kitten’s first vaccine to a senior dog’s most delicate surgery.

Our team blends thoughtful, gentle handling with the latest veterinary medicine. We take time to listen, explain options clearly, and partner with you on a plan that fits your pet and your life.

  • Fear-free, low-stress handling techniques
  • Transparent estimates — no surprises
  • Same-day appointments often available
  • CareCredit financing accepted
